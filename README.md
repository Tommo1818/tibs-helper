## 🎯 Purpose of This App

This revision hub provides interactive visualisations, theoretical debate summaries, real-time case study sorters, and self-scoring Level 7 marking rubrics across the first half of the unit (**Themes 0 through 4**):

* **Theme 0:** *Don't Believe The Hype!* (Epistemic vigilance, questioning technological hype, mental toolboxes).
* **Theme 1:** *Technology & Innovation* (Carlota Perez's 5 Great Surges & Two-Phase Wave vs. Clayton Christensen's Disruptive vs. Sustaining Innovation + Level 7 critiques from Birkinshaw, Lepore, Shaughnessy, and Edgerton).
* **Theme 2:** *Beyond Innovation* (Everett Rogers' 5 Perceived Attributes S-Curve simulator vs. Geoffrey Moore's High-Tech Chasm & Beachhead Strategy; Govindarajan & Trimble's Performance Engine vs. Dedicated Team + Retail Bank ML Loan Pre-Approval case sorter).
* **Theme 3:** *Organising & Financing Innovation* (Centralised R&D Labs at Bell Labs / Xerox PARC vs. Henry Chesbrough's Open Innovation; Mariana Mazzucato's *Entrepreneurial State* vs. Stian Westlake; Eric Ries's *Lean Startup* vs. Reid Hoffman's *Blitzscaling*).
* **Theme 4:** *Legal Matters* (Patents, Copyright, Trade Secrets + Start-Up IP Audit Sorter; Open Source Cathedral & Bazaar vs. Nadia Eghbal's *Working in Public*; UK GDPR & DUAA 2025 Meaningful Human Review; EU AI Act 4 Risk Tiers, Provider vs. Deployer duties, and the Teichmann vs. Ebers proportionality debate).
* **Mock Exams & Flashcards:** All 5 official Bristol exam prompts, 10 active recall flashcards, and interactive Level 7 rubric self-graders.

## 🚀 Running Locally

The app is 100% self-contained with zero external dependencies (no CDNs, no npm packages, pure vanilla JS and CSS):
1. Clone this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Tommo1818/tibs-helper.git
   ```
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
3. Switch between dark and light themes using the `☀️ / 🌙 Mode` button in the top-right corner.
