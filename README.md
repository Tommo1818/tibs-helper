## Purpose of This Repository

This revision hub provides interactive SVG visualisations, academic theory syntheses, real-time case study sorters, and self-scoring Level 7 marking rubrics across the first half of the unit (**Themes 0 through 4**):

* **Theme 0 & Theme 1 (`Tech & Innovation`):**
  * *Epistemic Vigilance:* Questioning vendor hype ("Don't Believe The Hype!") with competing analytical lenses.
  * *Carlota Perez (2002):* Interactive explorer for the 5 Great Surges (1771–1971) and the Two-Phase Wave (Installation Period / Financial Capital / Creative Destruction vs. Turning Point Crash vs. Deployment Period / Production Capital / Golden Age).
  * *Clayton Christensen (1997):* Interactive Sustaining vs. Disruptive trajectory graph, canonical empirical cases (Hard Disk Drives 14" to 3.5", Minimill Steel rebar to sheet steel), performance oversupply, and the autonomous spin-off prescription.
  * *Level 7 Critiques:* Julian Birkinshaw's 3 Myths of Disruption and 2x2 Strategic Response Matrix (*Retrench, Move Away, Double Down, Fight Back*), Jill Lepore (survivorship bias, Seagate), Haydn Shaughnessy (iPhone platform ecosystem anomaly & tautology trap), Henderson & Clark (modular vs. architectural innovation), and David Edgerton (*The Shock of the Old*, maintenance, and terminating unviable R&D projects early).
* **Theme 2 (`Beyond Innovation`):**
  * *Everett Rogers (1962) vs. Geoffrey Moore (1991):* Interactive adopter bell curve mapping Rogers' 5 categories to Moore's high-tech labels (*Techies, Visionaries, Pragmatists, Conservatives, Sceptics*), the 16% Chasm, the D-Day Beachhead & Whole Product strategy (*Early Market -> Chasm -> Bowling Alley -> Tornado -> Main Street*), Debi Andrus's critique, and a real-time 5 Perceived Attributes S-Curve simulator.
  * *Vijay Govindarajan & Chris Trimble (2010):* Performance Engine vs. Dedicated Team theory, disciplined experimentation (*Hypothesis of Record*, learning velocity), and an interactive 6-part Retail Bank ML Loan Pre-Approval capability sorter.
* **Theme 3 (`Organising & Financing Innovation`):**
  * *Organising R&D:* Centralised corporate labs (Bell Labs, Xerox PARC's GUI failure via core rigidity, Henderson & Clark architectural filtering, and Teece's complementary assets/appropriability) vs. Henry Chesbrough's Open Innovation (inbound/outbound flows, A&D) and O'Reilly & Tushman's Structural Ambidexterity.
  * *Financing Innovation:* TRL 1–9 capital sources, the "Valley of Death", and Mariana Mazzucato's *Entrepreneurial State* (DARPA/NSF funding the iPhone's core stack) vs. Stian Westlake's critique (intangible assets, state commercial flops like Concorde, downstream execution risk).
  * *Startup Scaling:* Guy Kawasaki (*Art of the Start 2.0*, DICEE), Eric Ries (*The Lean Startup*, Build–Measure–Learn MVP), and Reid Hoffman (*Blitzscaling*) vs. Kuratko, Holt & Neubert (2020) (*Flexport* vs. *WeWork, Uber, Zenefits, Theranos*).
* **Theme 4 (`Legal Matters, GDPR/DUAA & EU AI Act`):**
  * *Intellectual Property:* Patents (Utilitarian bargain, *software as such* vs. technical effect, Brin & Page paper vs. US6285999B1, Heller & Eisenberg's Tragedy of the Anticommons), Copyright (idea-expression dichotomy, *Temple Island v New English Teas [2012]* Red Bus case, *Google v. Oracle*), Trade Secrets (*Faccenda Chicken [1986]*, *Vestergaard [2013]*), and an interactive Start-Up IP Audit sorter (Seminar Slide 8).
  * *Open Source Governance:* Eric Raymond's *Cathedral and the Bazaar* (Linus's Law) vs. Nadia Eghbal's *Working in Public* (Federation vs. Stadium topologies; *Heartbleed*, *Log4j*, and *XZ Utils* crises), plus Permissive (MIT/Apache 2.0) vs. Copyleft (GPL) and Creative Commons (`CC BY-NC`) licensing.
  * *Data Protection & AI Regulation:* UK GDPR Art. 5 principles, the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA) mandatory safeguards for solely automated decisions on ordinary personal data, the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) 4 Risk Tiers & Provider vs. Deployer duties, and the Teichmann vs. Martin Ebers (2024) proportionality debate applied to all three seminar scenarios (Retailer Account Closure, EU Road Traffic AI, and University Admissions AI).
* **Mock Exams, Past Paper & Active Recall Deck:**
  * Explicit **Essential vs. Recommended / Optional** reading breakdowns across Themes 1–4 (where only Essential materials are mandatory to learn for the exam).
  * The official **Sample Past Year Paper Question & Marking Guide** on Carlota Perez's 5 Technological Revolutions and Financial Phases (20 points) plus all 6 official seminar and mock exam prompts with an auto-saving practice pad, self-timer, 12-card active recall flashcard deck, and interactive Level 7 marking rubric checklists.

## Running Locally

The application is 100% self-contained in a single `index.html` file with zero external runtime dependencies:
1. Clone this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Tommo1818/tibs-helper.git
   ```
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Toggle between Dark and Light modes using the top-right theme button.
